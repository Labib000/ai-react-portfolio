A Dubai rental company needed a fast, polished site where customers can find the exact luxury or sports car they want and send an enquiry straight away.

## What I built

Backend and frontend modules for the vehicle catalogue: brand and category browsing, search, and the REST APIs behind them, built with Laravel, React and MySQL.

## Outcome

A live rental platform serving customers across Dubai.
