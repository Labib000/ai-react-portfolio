## The problem

Every content change on the college website needed a developer, and there was no approval step for departments.

## What I built

Custom WordPress plugins and post types for department content, a Laravel admin panel for profiles, and approval workflows for the Principal, Director and HODs.

## Outcome

Staff with no coding background now publish content themselves, and publishing got about 30 to 40% faster.
