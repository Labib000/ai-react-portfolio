## The problem

Publishing campaigns meant logging in to each platform by hand, with no approval step and no record of who changed what.

## What I built

A Laravel console with approval workflows for admins, authors and approvers, notifications, audit trails, Facebook Graph API publishing and FFmpeg video processing.

## Outcome

Automated scheduling and publishing cut manual campaign work by about 25 to 30%.
